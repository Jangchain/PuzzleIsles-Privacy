谜屿隐私政策

English

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 25 日

App 内的数据

谜屿（Puzzle Isles）是由 Jangchain 发布的离线解谜游戏合集。App 无需注册或登录，不包含广告、分析或跟踪 SDK，也不会将游戏数据发送给开发者。游戏进度、最佳成绩、音频与触感设置等信息保存在设备上，用来恢复游戏状态和偏好。

旧版的照片导入和分享

旧版若包含关卡工坊，在导入二维码时，App 通过 Apple 的系统照片选择器读取你主动选中的图片，在设备上识别二维码。App 不上传或保存原始图片。成功导入时，关卡内容会作为本地草稿保存。你也可以通过系统分享菜单主动发送关卡码或二维码，分享对象和应用由你选择。接收方或分享服务按各自的规则处理你发送的内容。

保留和删除

游戏数据保存在 App 的本地存储中。可以使用系统的「删除 App」移除 App 和设备上随 App 保存的数据。若已有系统备份或曾主动分享关卡，可分别通过系统备份设置或接收服务管理这些副本。

外部网页和联系

App 设置中的链接由浏览器打开。此政策页面由 GitHub Pages 托管，GitHub 可能处理 IP 地址等访问数据以提供和保护服务。请参阅 GitHub 隐私声明。如有隐私问题或请求，请通过 政策仓库的问题页面联系开发者。问题页面公开，请勿贴出个人信息或私密资料。

Puzzle Isles Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Data in the app

Puzzle Isles is an offline puzzle collection published by Jangchain. It requires no account, contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and sends no game data to the developer. Game progress, high scores, and audio and haptic preferences are stored on your device to restore game state and preferences.

Photo import and sharing in earlier versions

In earlier versions with the level workshop, when you import a QR code, the app reads the image you select through Apple’s photo picker and scans it on your device. The app does not upload or save the original image. A valid imported level is saved locally as a draft. You can choose to share a level code or QR image using the system share sheet. The recipient or service you choose handles the shared content under its own terms.

Retention and deletion

Game data is kept in the app’s local storage. Use the system’s Delete App option to remove the app and its data from your device. If you have an existing device backup or shared a level, you can manage those copies separately in your backup settings or with the receiving service.

External pages and contact

Links in the app’s settings open in your browser. This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process access data such as IP addresses to provide and protect its services. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through the policy repository’s issues page. Issues are public, so please keep personal or confidential information out of your post.
